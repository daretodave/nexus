A CI workflow wakes nexus on a schedule. Each run claims the next chunk, builds it, checks it and commits, then parks any question for the morning. Same loop, minus your laptop.

  • Questions from cloud runs land in your oversight inbox.
  • Pause it any time from the top bar or Settings.
